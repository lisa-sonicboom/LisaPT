01 — Approach

No cookie-cutter templates.
No one-size-fits-all.

Every client arrives with a different body, history and goal. The training reflects that, from the first session to the hundredth.

i.

Listen first

We start with your story: injuries, sleep, stress, current schedule, what's worked and what's burned you out before.

ii.

Assess, then program

A light movement screen shows me where you're strong, where you have restrictions, and where to start. Your plan is written for your body.

iii.

Train with intent

Sessions are focused and technical. You'll know why every exercise is there and what it's doing for you.

iv.

Adjust, always

Life changes. Your program changes with it. We review every few weeks and recalibrate so progress keeps stacking.

02 — Training

What we can work on together.

A mix, personalised to you. Pick one or blend two or three of these.

Strength & Body Composition

Build real strength and reshape how you look and feel. Progressive, sustainable, no crash diets.

Mobility & Posture

Undo what desks and long periods of sitting have done. Move better, sit without aching, stand taller.

Conditioning & HIIT

Intervals, high-intensity, heart and lung work. Dialled to your level, not in a bootcamp style.

Return to Training

Coming back from injury or a long pause, or a new city? We'll rebuild the base carefully.

Longevity & Everyday Strength

Train for the next thirty years, not the next thirty days. Joints, balance, resilience, energy.

Calisthenics & Skills

Pull-ups, handstands, levers. Bodyweight skill work for the curious. Strength you can show.

06 — FAQ

Good questions.

What actually happens in the free session?

We'll sit down for 10–15 minutes to talk about your goals, history and any limitations. Then a short movement assessment, followed by a proper training block so you get a real feel for how I coach. You leave with clear thoughts on what a plan would look like. No pressure to commit.

Where do sessions take place?

In Bonfim, Porto. Exact location details are shared once your free session is booked.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. Many of my clients haven't trained seriously before, or are coming back after years away. Personal training is arguably more useful when you're starting. We get the foundations right so nothing hurts later.

I'm already advanced. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Advanced clients tend to need sharper programming, better recovery structure and an outside eye on technique. That's exactly what I can help you with.

Do you offer online coaching?

Yes. I can build you a personalised program delivered through my coaching app, or run live sessions over Zoom. Prices are adjusted accordingly. Message me for details.

Do you take couples or duo sessions?

Yes. Training with a partner or friend can be a great way to stay consistent and split the cost. Duo sessions are programmed for both of you and priced per pair. Get in touch for current rates.

Do you speak English? Portuguese?

Both. Sessions can be in either English, Portuguese or Finnish.

How often should I train?

Most clients benefit most from 2 sessions per week. We'll decide together based on your goals, schedule, budget and what your body can recover from.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations or reschedules with at least 24 hours' notice are free. Later than that, the session is charged; fair to both of us.
