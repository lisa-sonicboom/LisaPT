01 — Approach

No templates.
No guesswork.

Every client arrives with a different body, history and goal. The training reflects that — from the first session to the hundredth.

i.

Listen first

We start with your story — injuries, sleep, stress, schedule, what's worked and what's burned you out. Programming without this is guessing.

ii.

Assess, then program

A light movement screen shows me where you're strong, where you're locked up, and where to start. Your plan is written for your body.

iii.

Train with intent

Sessions are focused and technical, not chaotic. You'll know why every exercise is there and what it's doing for you.

iv.

Adjust, always

Life changes. Your program changes with it. We review every few weeks and recalibrate so progress keeps stacking.

05 — FAQ

Good questions.

What actually happens in the free session?

We'll sit down for 10–15 minutes to talk about your goals, history and any limitations. Then a short movement assessment, followed by a proper training block so you get a real feel for how I coach. You leave with clear thoughts on what a plan would look like — no pressure to commit.

Where do sessions take place?

In Bonfim, Porto. Exact location details are shared once your free session is booked.

I'm a total beginner. Is this for me?

Yes. A lot of my clients haven't trained seriously before, or are coming back after years away. Personal training is arguably more useful when you're starting — we get the foundations right so nothing hurts later.

I'm already advanced. Can you still help?

Absolutely. Advanced clients tend to need sharper programming, better recovery structure and an outside eye on technique. That's exactly what 1:1 is for.

Do you speak English? Portuguese?

Both. Sessions can be in either — whichever you're more comfortable with.

How often should I train?

Most clients do 2–3 sessions per week. We'll decide together based on your goals, schedule and what your body can recover from.

What's your cancellation policy?

Cancellations or reschedules with at least 24 hours' notice are free. Later than that, the session is charged — fair to both of us.
